SELECTED WORK / 01

Health systems

Helping institutions understand how care works in practice, where access breaks down, and what it takes to improve delivery.

First Nations Health Authority · 2024–2025

First Nations health governance

Managed the evaluation of FNHA and the BC Tripartite Framework Agreement, coordinating a review of 1,800 documents, 75 interviews, 28 engagement sessions, a 315-response survey, and more than 30 technical reports. Findings were validated with partners and regional leaders.

Governance · engagement · accountability

Sioux Lookout First Nations Health Authority · 2024

An oral health business case for 33 communities

Led engagement in seven fly-in communities and developed a costed business case for oral health services. The work combined international comparisons with service options, financial modelling, operational requirements, partnerships, and funding mechanisms.

Access to care · finance · implementation

BC Ministry of Mental Health and Addictions · 2023–2025

BC decriminalization policy evaluation

Managed study design, research, and engagement on a highly scrutinized policy file. Brought together evidence from health authorities, police, local governments, Indigenous communities, and people who use drugs to inform implementation and policy improvement.

Public policy · complex evidence · stakeholder engagement

BC Ministry of Mental Health and Addictions · 2022–2025

Integrated Child and Youth teams

Managed a three-year implementation evaluation of the ICY team model. Worked with ministry representatives, healthcare providers, teams, and community partners to examine operations and early outcomes and support continuous improvement.

Mental health · integrated services · performance

First Nations and Inuit Health Branch · Doctors of BC · Yukon

Clinical care & quality improvement

Experience includes evaluation of the First Nations and Inuit Health Branch Clinical Care Program, physician quality improvement, and Yukon’s continuous glucose monitoring pilot. Across these assignments, the work connected frontline experience with evidence about service delivery and outcomes.

Clinical services · quality · patient experience

Social finance · performance readiness

Canadian Race Relations Foundation · 2025–2026

Organization-wide performance & data

Arctic Inspiration Prize · 2021–2025

Community-led impact in the North

Impact assessment · community perspectives
