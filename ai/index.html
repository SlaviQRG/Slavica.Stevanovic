MY APPROACH TO ADOPTION

Start with how knowledge moves.

Helping an organization adopt AI begins with its people and processes. I map how knowledge is created, shared, checked, and used, then identify where a new capability would make the work meaningfully better.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Listen to the people doing the work. Map decisions, information, handoffs, and recurring friction.

  2. 02

    Design

    Choose a useful problem. Define the role of AI, the evidence it needs, and the human review points.

  3. 03

    Experiment

    Test a bounded workflow with real examples. Check accuracy, traceability, and usefulness.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Measure what improves, document what fails, and refine the process with the people who use it.
